Our investment portfolio philosophy to focus on bullet and locked out securities in order to have certainty of cash flows continues to pay off. The unrealized loss in our AFS portfolio improved 6% sequentially and despite the 10-year treasury rate being a few basis points higher than the prior quarter end. The AOCI burndown will continue to benefit tangible book value per share growth as these positions pull to par. Now diving further into the income statement.
