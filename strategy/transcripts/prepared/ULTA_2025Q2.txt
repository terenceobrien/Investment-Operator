The increase was largely due to lower inventory shrink and higher merchandise margin, which was partially offset by the deleverage of supply chain fixed costs and other revenue. Fixture investments, process improvements, associate training and specific store level actions are driving improved trends. Store payroll and benefit expense increased primarily due to higher healthcare costs and additional selling hours to support the guest experience. As a percent of sales, operating margin decreased 50 basis points to 12.4% of sales compared to 12.9% last year.
