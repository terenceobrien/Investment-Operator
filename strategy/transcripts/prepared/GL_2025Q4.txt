Now with regards to our full year underwriting margins, normalized for the impact of assumption updates. As I mentioned on previous calls, as required by GAAP accounting standards, each year, we review and generally update actuarial assumptions for mortality, morbidity and lapses, and we have chosen to do this in the third quarter each year. When assumptions changes are made, GAAP accounting standards require a cumulative catch-up adjustment. This cumulative catch-up is the assumption related remeasurement gain or loss, an assumption remeasurement gain lowers the reserve balances and indicates an improved outlook as less premium is needed to fund reserves to meet future policy obligations. The opposite is true if there is an assumption remeasurement loss. To better understand the performance of the business for the full year, we think it is beneficial to look at normalized underwriting margins, which exclude the impact of assumption changes and provide an improved basis for comparison of year-over-year results.
