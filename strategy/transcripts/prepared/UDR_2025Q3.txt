To begin, third quarter year-over-year same-store revenue and NOI growth of 2.6% and 2.3%, respectively, exceeded consensus expectations and were driven by: first, 0.8% blended lease rate growth, which was a result of renewal rate growth of 3.3% and new lease rate growth of negative 2.6%. Collectively, expenses across these 3 categories, which account for nearly 2/3 of total expenses, grew a mere 1.9%. Based on our year-to-date results through the third quarter and recognizing the trends we have experienced thus far to begin the fourth quarter, we adjusted our full year 2025 same-store revenue growth guidance midpoint to 2.4% from 2.5% previously.
