For the full year, net revenue grew 14% to $4.7 billion, driven by exceptional performance in Wizards and continued progress across the rest of the portfolio. Adjusted operating profit increased 36% to $1.1 billion with an adjusted operating margin of 24.2%, up nearly 400 basis points versus last year, driven by favorable mix and cost productivity. Adjusted earnings per diluted share were $5.54. For the full year, Wizards' revenue increased 45% to $2.2 billion with operating profit of just over $1 billion and an operating margin of 46%. Entertainment performed in line with expectations for the quarter and the year, delivering stable revenue and adjusted margins consistent with our asset-light strategy.
