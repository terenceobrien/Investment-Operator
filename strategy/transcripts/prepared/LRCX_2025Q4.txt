AI is driving greater transistor performance requirements and, in turn, accelerating the inflection to gate-all-around device architectures. However, below 2 nanometers, gate-all-around structures begin to encounter significant resistance capacitance or RC challenges. Narrower transistor contacts in these devices cause greater electrons gathering, resulting in higher resistance of the deposited tungsten films. Replacing tungsten with molybdenum solves the resistance problem, but the process of depositing this higher performance material is inherently slower and more complex. This is driving a roughly 3x increase in Lam metal deposition SAM per wafer when transitioning to advanced gate-all-around nodes.
