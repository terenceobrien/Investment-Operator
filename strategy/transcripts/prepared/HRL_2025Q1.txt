Jacinth Smiley: Thank you, Jim. And good morning, everyone. As Jim noted, we achieved solid top-line results in the first quarter and made great progress on our strategic priorities. Interest and investment income for the first quarter decreased, primarily due to lower performance from the Rabbi Trust. The effective tax rate was 21.8%, which is favorable compared to the prior year due to the purchase of federal transferable energy credits. We are committed to dividend growth and remain a proud dividend aristocrat.
