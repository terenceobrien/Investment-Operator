Adjusted earnings per diluted share were $1.47, up 41% year-over-year, reflecting strong operating leverage and disciplined execution. Looking more closely at the segments, Wizards momentum continued. Segment revenue grew 26% to $582 million behind the strength in Magic. Product mix and scale were more than able to offset the headwind of higher royalty and operating expense. Digital and licensing revenue was up 3% and MONOPOLY Go delivered $41 million of revenue, in line with our expectations. Consumer Products revenue was $398 million, essentially flat year-over-year with growth in toy and game volume, offset by a decline in licensing as we lap challenging prior year comparison. Adjusted operating loss was $41 million, a decline of roughly $10 million versus last year on an adjusted basis. The loss reflects higher royalty expense, incremental tariffs, and the impact of prior year licensing strength.
