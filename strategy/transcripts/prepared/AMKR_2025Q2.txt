We ended the quarter with a stronger balance sheet and greater financial flexibility. Amkor is focused on creating long-term shareholder value through a balanced and disciplined capital allocation strategy with 4 key priorities: One, investing in organic growth, by expanding our manufacturing footprint and advanced packaging capabilities; two, making selective strategic investments to enable regional supply chain or for Tuck-in opportunities; three, maintaining balance sheet strength and flexibility; and four, returning capital to shareholders within our established framework of returning 40% to 50% of Free Cash Flow over time. To further strengthen our balance sheet and liquidity, we replaced our $600 million credit agreement with a new $1 billion revolver and executed a $500 million term loan.
